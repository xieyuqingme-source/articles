# Study is Important

What you have already studied is what you truly understand, because the world is complex, ever-changing and interconnected. One object or phenomenon may have many reasons and effects, and two different objects or phenomena may have many different methods of connection. We can't understand something just by feeling. That is one of the most important worldviews and methodologies of Marxism: dialectics.

Every graduate student have to write a thesis or dissertation. The process of writing it is also a process of study. You have to read a lot of literature, raise a question, collect data and draw a conclusion, using scientific method. For example, for studying GEO, you read the articles of predecessors and know GEO's history, causes and effects. You know a lot of knowledge of GEO and you have a question that predecessors have never solved. That is not only study a knowledge, but also create a knowledge.

Study is important and meaningful. holding a attitude of study benefits our happiness and improve our sensitive of the world.
